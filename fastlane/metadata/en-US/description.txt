Room for Two keeps one pretend picnic blanket on the phone. It starts empty. The kid, Grandma, or Grandpa can sit. Two seats are the limit. A third person stays standing. The last person who sat is the one who stands. The huge line says the blanket is empty, there is room for one more, or the blanket is full.

The blanket stays on this phone. Start a new blanket when you want both seats empty again.

Room for Two does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. It does not reserve a real blanket. The blanket in the app is original.
